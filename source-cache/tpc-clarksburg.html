Considerations to care for your truck in Clarksburg, WV seasons and weather

If you're planning semi truck parking in Clarksburg, WV, keep the season in view before you set up for the night. Weather shifts are real here, and the road can change under you fast.

  • Spring (March to May): Temperatures warm from the mid-50s in March to the mid-70s by May. Rain is common, so watch for slick ramps, darker braking zones, and extra stopping distance.
  • Summer (June to August): Days are warm and humid, often in the mid to upper 80s. Thunderstorms hit often in the afternoons and evenings, bringing sudden downpours and lightning that can slow your move and tighten parking plans.
  • Fall (September to November): Cooler air and lower humidity make fall easier to drive, but early fall can still give you warm days. By late October, temperatures usually drop into the 50s, and early snowfall can show up in November.
  • Winter (December to February): Expect cold weather, often below freezing. Snowfall is common, averaging about 24 inches per season, and ice on the road is a major hazard if you're heading in for tractor-trailer parking late.

Check weather updates often before you roll into or out of Clarksburg. That helps you time big rig parking and avoid getting stuck hunting for a spot after the weather turns.
