Beckley travel plaza

Location
500 Vankirk Dr.
Beckley, West Virginia 25801
(I-77 Exit 45, northbound and southbound, West Virgin​ia Turnpike)

Phone: (304) 2​56-6694

Information
Your stop for food, fuel, and travel convenience on the West Virginia Turnpike.

  • Starbucks, open 6:00 AM to 8:00 PM.
  • Wendy’s, open 24 hours with the exception of Sunday nights.
  • Popeye's, open 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM.
  • Mountain State Market, open 24 hours, an expanded convenience and grab-and-go hub offering regional foods, travel essentials, and West Virginia made products.
  • Food Truck, open 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM. 
  • The Tamarack Artisan Shop, celebrating West Virginia craftsmanship through locally produced art, gifts, and specialty goods!

Amenities

  • Clean, spacious restrooms
  • Indoor seating areas
  • Expanded parking for cars and trucks
  • Separate fueling areas for passenger vehicles and commercial trucks
  • 8 EV fast charging stations
  • Travel information and visitor guidance
  • Well-lit walkways and parking areas

Bluestone travel plaza

Location
Milepost 18, Northbound Access Only
I-77, West Virginia Turnpike
Princeton, West Virginia 24740

Phone: (304) 384-7535

Information

  • Your stop for food, fuel, and travel convenience on the West Virginia Turnpike.
    • Wendy’s, open 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM, offering fast and familiar meals for travelers.
    • Mountain State Market, open 24 hours, featuring grab-and-go foods, travel essentials, and West Virginia-made products. 
    • Exxon fuel services, including gasoline, diesel, and a satellite diesel fuel island for commercial drivers.   

Amenities

  • Clean restrooms 
  • 24-hour operation with friendly onsite service 
  • Parking areas for cars and trucks 
  • Drive-through service for added convenience 
  • 4 EV charging stations (planned and in development) 
  • Travel information and assistance from counselors 
  • Well-lit, safe environment for day and night travel

princeton welcome center

Location
Exit 9, I77
West Virginia Turnpike
Princeton, West Virginia

Phone: (304) 487-2214
Fax: (304) 487-2130

Hours: 8:30 AM to 6:30 PM

Services Available

  • Professional Travel Counselors available to assist with statewide travel information
  • Guidance on local theater performances, festivals, and special events
  • Free Wi-Fi
  • Free West Virginia state maps (also available at all Turnpike Travel Plazas)
  • AAA and AARP discounts honored at West Virginia Made craft shops
  • Discount travel guides with coupons for nearby attractions

Features

  • West Virginia Made crafts, including quilts, pottery, glassware, wooden items, and local foods
  • Public rest area facilities with comfortable indoor space
  • Beautiful, landscaped gardens and outdoor areas
  • Sheltered picnic area
  • Vietnam Memorial Monument
  • Weather information
  • Public telephone
  • Television
  • Vending room
  • ATM on site
  • Buses welcome
