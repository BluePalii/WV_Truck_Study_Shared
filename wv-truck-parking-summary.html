Executive summary

This study inventoried 59 locations relevant to semi-truck parking on the four target corridors, spanning 17 West Virginia counties from the Kentucky, Virginia, Ohio, and Pennsylvania lines. Of these, 42 are operating facilities with designated truck parking, providing an estimated ~1357 truck spaces (460 public, 855 at private truck stops, 42 in paid reservable lots). The remainder are informal/big-box locations (~24+ undesignated spaces), satellite-identified candidate sites (~90+ potential spaces), fuel-only stops, and resolved closures or directory ghosts. Three sites were field-verified by the study team in July 2026; all other counts derive from operator publications, directories, or satellite imagery analysis and are flagged accordingly.

Directional access

17 facilities are single-direction only — all of them public: EB-only (I-64 Welcome Center East, Hurricane Rest Area East), WB-only (Hurricane West, I-64 Welcome Center West), NB-only (Bluestone Travel Plaza, Morton, Mineral Wells NB, Servia NB, Burnsville NB, Meadowbrook NB), and SB-only (Camp Creek Wayside, Burnwell, Mineral Wells SB, Servia SB, Burnsville SB, Meadowbrook SB, I-79 SB Welcome Center). Every private truck stop offers both-direction access via its exit. Beckley Travel Plaza serves both directions via the Exit 45 ramps despite sitting on the SB side.

Key findings and coverage gaps

  • Morton Travel Plaza is demolished (rebuild contract completion Oct 31, 2026): zero interim spaces at NB MM 72. NB Turnpike trucks have no public facility between Beckley MM 45 and the GoMart at Exit 95 — a 50-mile gap with only Burnwell (SB-only) between.
  • I-64 east of Nitro is nearly empty: Alta Truck Plaza (Exit 161, field-verified open, ~19 spaces) is the only truck stop between Pilot Nitro (Exit 45) and the Virginia line outside the Turnpike. One directory wrongly lists Alta as closed.
  • Known congestion: WVDOT's 2023 Truck Parking Profile flags Pilot Morgantown as "always full" and TA Hurricane / Pilot Nitro as "typically very congested." Pilot #474 Sutton is frequently cited as filling overnight.
  • Servia rest areas (I-79 MM 49, both directions) have a $21M renovation contract awarded June 30, 2026 — expect closures during construction; they are the nearest public parking to the north end of US-19 Corridor L.
  • Turnpike rebuilds complete except Morton: Beckley Travel Plaza reopened Dec 30, 2024 with 165 truck spaces (largest lot on the corridor, +35); Bluestone reopened mid-Jan 2025 with 79 (+10).
  • Directory ghosts and stale data resolved: "I-77 Truck Stop Princeton" (Exit 9) does not exist; Camp Creek Exxon's 104-space listing was stale data from old Bluestone; GoMart Parkersburg's 10-space claim is unsupported by imagery; Go-Mart #33 Fairplain's 15-space claim is doubtful on imagery.
  • Formalization candidates found by satellite: US-19/I-79 Exit 57 gravel pads (strongest — two ~200×250 ft pads, 40–60 semis, visible gore-area parking nearby), Mink Shoals I-79 Exit 1 frontage yard (~25–35 trucks, WVDOT-flagged area), Hico US-19/US-60 yard, and Sam Black Church I-64 Exit 156 pads.
  • Largest facilities: Beckley Travel Plaza (165, public), U-Save/Mabel's Mount Nebo (~100–150, largest on Corridor L), TA Mineral Wells (104), Love's Ripley (80), Bluestone (79), TA Hurricane (76), TA Jane Lew (75), Pilot Sutton (67).
  • Princeton Lowe's/Walmart informal parking is excluded by study ground rules; Princeton public facilities (Bluestone, Camp Creek Wayside, Princeton Welcome Center) are included.
