  • I-77 free section Exits 99-124: ~35-mile stretch with zero documented facilities; Ripley Exit 138 has only car-oriented stations.
  • I-79 Mink Shoals (Exits 1-9): WVDOT documents recurring undesignated truck parking. July 2026 satellite sweep of Exits 0-2 found one linear truck/trailer yard NW of Exit 1 (~25-35 units; see Unresolved card) and no other truck-capable site; ramps and shoulders remain the only informal option — field-check.
  • I-79 Exits 91-99 (Weston): July 2026 satellite scan of the Weston / US-33 area found no truck-capable facility; between the Burnsville rest areas (MM 84) and TA Jane Lew (Exit 105) there is no commercial truck parking on this corridor.
  • I-79 Clarksburg/Bridgeport Exits 119-125 and Fairmont/Morgantown Exits 132-155: no branded truck stop despite heavy commercial development (except K&T Exit 139 and Pilot Exit 146, both small relative to demand).
  • US-19 south half (Beckley to Oak Hill, ~20 mi): zero documented truck parking; only paid reservable lots off-corridor in Oak Hill.
  • US-19 Hico (US-60 junction): Sunoco + truck repair yard; suspected informal parking, undocumented.
  • US-19 at I-79 Exit 57: truckers report shoulder/ramp parking at the junction; no facility exists.
